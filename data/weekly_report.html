中文摘要整理
研究目的 本世代研究旨在探討To evaluate the associations of free T, total T, and sex hormone-binding globulin (SHBG) with incident frailty among MWH.
研究方法 Three hundred six nonfrail MWH (129 robust and 177 prefrail) with longitudinal frailty assessments from the Multicenter AIDS Cohort Study and Women's Interagency HIV Study Combined Cohort Study were enrolled. Incident frailty defined using the Fried frailty phenotype.
主要結果 Over a mean follow-up of 9.0 years, low free T (<54 pg/mL) and low total T (<300 ng/dL) were associated with higher risk of incident frailty compared with higher concentrations (hazard ratio [HR]: 2.49, 95% CI: 1.21-5.11; HR: 3.31, 95% CI: 1.26-8.68, respectively).
結論 Low T levels predict incident frailty in MWH. Assessment of androgen status may help identify men at increased frailty risk. Further investigations are warranted to assess the role of T therapy in frailty prevention.
